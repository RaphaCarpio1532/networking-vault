# Man in the Middle

## 🇬🇧 English
**MITM**: an attacker secretly relays/alters traffic between two parties (e.g. via [[ARP Poisoning]] or rogue Wi-Fi). Lets them read or change data. Defense: [[Encryption]], [[TLS and SSL]], [[VPN]], certificate validation.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
**MITM**: un attaccante intercetta/altera di nascosto il traffico tra due parti (es. tramite [[ARP Poisoning]] o Wi-Fi malevolo). Difesa: [[Encryption]], [[TLS and SSL]], [[VPN]].
