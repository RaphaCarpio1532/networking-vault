# ARP

## 🇬🇧 English
**Address Resolution Protocol** maps an IP address to a [[MAC Address]] on a local network. The device broadcasts "who has this IP?" and stores the answer in the ARP table. Because it trusts any reply, it is abused in [[ARP Poisoning]] and [[Man in the Middle]] attacks.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
**Address Resolution Protocol** associa un indirizzo IP a un [[MAC Address]] nella rete locale. Poiché si fida di qualsiasi risposta, viene sfruttato negli attacchi di [[ARP Poisoning]] e [[Man in the Middle]].
