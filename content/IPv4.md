# IPv4

## 🇬🇧 English
32-bit address written as four octets (e.g. 192.168.1.10). ~4.3 billion addresses, now exhausted — a key reason for [[NAT and PAT]] and [[IPv6]]. Paired with a subnet mask to split network vs host portion.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Indirizzo a 32 bit scritto come quattro ottetti (es. 192.168.1.10). Circa 4,3 miliardi di indirizzi, ormai esauriti — motivo chiave di [[NAT and PAT]] e [[IPv6]].
