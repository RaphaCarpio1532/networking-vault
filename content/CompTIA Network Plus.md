# CompTIA Network Plus

## 🇬🇧 English
Vendor-neutral entry cert covering the [[OSI Model]], [[IP Addressing]], [[Routing]], [[Switches]], wireless and basic security. Ideal next step after help desk to prove networking skills. Often taken before [[CompTIA Security Plus]].

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Certificazione entry-level neutrale che copre [[OSI Model]], [[IP Addressing]], [[Routing]], [[Switches]], wireless e sicurezza di base. Passo ideale dopo l'help desk. Spesso presa prima di [[CompTIA Security Plus]].
