# Routers

## 🇬🇧 English
A Layer 3 device that connects different networks and forwards [[Packets and Frames]] between them using [[IP Addressing]] and a routing table. The home router also does [[NAT and PAT]], [[DHCP]] and basic [[Firewalls]].

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Un dispositivo di Livello 3 che collega reti diverse e inoltra i pacchetti usando l'[[IP Addressing]] e una tabella di routing. Il router domestico fa anche [[NAT and PAT]], [[DHCP]] e firewall di base.
