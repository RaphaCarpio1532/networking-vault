# VPN

## 🇬🇧 English
A **Virtual Private Network** creates an encrypted tunnel over a public network, securing remote access. Types: **IPsec** (site-to-site/client) and **SSL/TLS** (browser-based). Protects data in transit from [[Packet Sniffing]] and [[Man in the Middle]].

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Una **VPN** crea un tunnel cifrato su una rete pubblica, proteggendo l'accesso remoto. Tipi: **IPsec** e **SSL/TLS**. Protegge i dati in transito da [[Packet Sniffing]] e [[Man in the Middle]].
