# IPv6

## 🇬🇧 English
128-bit address written in hex (e.g. 2001:0db8::1). Practically unlimited addresses, built-in support for IPsec, no need for [[NAT and PAT]]. Adoption is growing; help desk should recognize the format.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Indirizzo a 128 bit scritto in esadecimale (es. 2001:0db8::1). Indirizzi praticamente illimitati, supporto integrato per IPsec, niente [[NAT and PAT]].
