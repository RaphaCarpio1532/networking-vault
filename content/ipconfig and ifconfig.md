# ipconfig and ifconfig

## 🇬🇧 English
Show and manage local IP configuration. Windows: `ipconfig /all`, `ipconfig /release`, `ipconfig /renew`, `ipconfig /flushdns`. Linux/macOS: `ifconfig` or `ip a`. Daily help-desk basics for checking IP, gateway and [[DHCP]] leases.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Mostrano e gestiscono la configurazione IP locale. Windows: `ipconfig /all`, `/release`, `/renew`, `/flushdns`. Linux/macOS: `ifconfig` o `ip a`. Basi quotidiane dell'help-desk.
