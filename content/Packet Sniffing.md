# Packet Sniffing

## 🇬🇧 English
Capturing network traffic to read its contents. A legitimate diagnostic ([[Wireshark]], [[tcpdump]]) but malicious on plaintext traffic. Why HTTPS/[[Encryption]] matters — encrypted packets are useless to a sniffer.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Catturare il traffico di rete per leggerne il contenuto. Diagnostica legittima ([[Wireshark]], [[tcpdump]]) ma malevola sul traffico in chiaro. Ecco perché contano HTTPS e [[Encryption]].
