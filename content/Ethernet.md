# Ethernet

## 🇬🇧 English
The dominant wired LAN technology (IEEE 802.3). Defines how [[Packets and Frames]] move over cables at Layer 1/2, using [[MAC Address]]es. Speeds from 100 Mbps to 100 Gbps.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
La tecnologia LAN cablata dominante (IEEE 802.3). Definisce come i [[Packets and Frames]] si muovono sui cavi ai Livelli 1/2, usando i [[MAC Address]].
