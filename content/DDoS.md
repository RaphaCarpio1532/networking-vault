# DDoS

## 🇬🇧 English
**Distributed Denial of Service**: many compromised machines (a botnet) flood a target with traffic until it is unavailable — attacking the **availability** part of the CIA triad. Defense: rate limiting, scrubbing services (Cloudflare), anycast.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
**Distributed Denial of Service**: molte macchine compromesse (una botnet) inondano un bersaglio di traffico fino a renderlo non disponibile — colpisce la **disponibilità**. Difesa: rate limiting, servizi di scrubbing.
