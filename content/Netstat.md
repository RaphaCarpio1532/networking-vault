# Netstat

## 🇬🇧 English
Shows active connections, listening [[Ports and Protocols]] and routing info on the local machine. `netstat -ano` (Windows) maps ports to process IDs — useful to spot suspicious connections. Modern Linux: `ss`.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano
Mostra connessioni attive, porte in ascolto e info di routing sulla macchina locale. `netstat -ano` (Windows) associa le porte ai processi — utile per individuare connessioni sospette. Linux moderno: `ss`.
